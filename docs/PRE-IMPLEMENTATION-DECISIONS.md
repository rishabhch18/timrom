## Baseline already confirmed

- One shared Angular codebase for both collaborators, faithfully matching the existing Timrom HTML graphics, layout, movement and animations while applying confirmed product-rule changes. Integrate with the existing server-authoritative backend behavior. Socializing is primary; study, work, gaming and other community types remain supported.
- Pilot target: 100 simultaneous online users using laptop, Android and iOS browsers; up to 100 simultaneous voice participants and 10 cameras across the app; minimum device profiles remain to be finalized. India, English/Hindi, web first; native mobile later. Age 13+ with separate teen/adult spaces. Approximately ₹2,000/month is an operating target, not a verified hosting quote.
- Either verified email or mobile; email/password or mobile OTP; unique username, separate display name, private contact details.
- Community homes first. Create home is optional and uses templates; no automatic personal house on signup. Basic customization is free.
- Current home has full detail. Other joined homes appear as small previews. Walking to a neighbour's gate or clicking for direct travel switches which home is detailed.
- Each home has a moderated outdoor forum, open to eligible visitors, with unlimited logical entry. Interior permissions are separate from forum visitation. Indoor rooms support positive capacities or 0 = unlimited.
- Each room's owner/admin chooses open conversation or moderated speakers. Listeners can text chat; speaking permissions are server-enforced.
- Voice auto-join asks once and remembers consent. Later entry joins automatically while enabled, even without a prior call. Mic/camera choices remain under user control.
- Click/tap-to-walk, connected doorways, angled following camera and whole-home overview. Furniture stays fixed during ordinary use. Object actions use a visual menu and believable motion/contacts.
- Separate room/furniture tracking opt-ins; private activity controls. Online time earns XP; interactive participation earns coins; XP unlocks eligibility and coins buy items. Earned coins come first; paid purchases follow later. Prices remain open.
- One furniture copy per purchase; reusable finishes. Personal ownership, permanent donation and reclaimable lending are included.
- External games first. Pets, NPC household, tasks, personal journal and music backends follow social homes/chat/calls. Basic activity history is distinct from the later personal journal feature.
